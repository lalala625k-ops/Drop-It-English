📲 How to share from other apps

🤖 Android:

1. Open this page in Chrome or your mobile browser.

2. Open the browser menu ⋮ and choose Add to Home screen or Install app.

3. After installation, use the Share menu in your photo library or browser and choose “Drop-It Companion” to send the item.

🍎 iPhone:

1. Open “Shortcuts”。

2. Create a shortcut that accepts images, text and URLs, and enable Show in Share Sheet.

3. Add Get Contents of URL and set the method to POST，with this address:http://desktop-ip:8088/api/share。

4. Share a screenshot to your shortcut to send it to the desktop.
